================================================================================
                         HOW TO USE
================================================================================

CREATE MEETING (AS HOST)
1. Open app
2. Fill "Create New Meeting" form
   - Host Name: Your name
   - Title: Meeting title
   - Description: Meeting details
3. Click "🚀 Create Meeting"
4. Share the meeting code with participants

JOIN MEETING (AS PARTICIPANT)
1. Open app
2. Fill "Join Existing Meeting" form
   - Meeting Code: 6-character code from host
   - Your Name: Your display name
3. Click "🚀 Join Meeting"

BROWSE & JOIN COURSES
1. Click "📚 Browse Courses" on home screen
2. Browse Live, Scheduled, or Completed tabs
3. Click on a course
4. Select role: Host or Participant
5. Enter your name
6. Click "Continue"

USE MEETING CONTROLS
📹 Video Button: Toggle camera (blue=on, red=off)
🎤 Audio Button: Toggle microphone (green=on, red=off)
🖥️ Share Button: Toggle screen sharing (orange=sharing)
💬 Chat Button: Toggle between video and chat view
📞 Leave Button: Exit the meeting
